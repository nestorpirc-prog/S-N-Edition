About the Game

Welcome to Golden Equity, the ultimate corporate empire builder. Start from zero and grow your wealth into trillions of dollars. Manage investments, acquire strategic businesses, and watch your income grow passively.

Core Features:

  • Dynamic Clicker Mechanic with Vibrate Feedback
  • Scalable Passive Income System
  • Strategic Business Acquisition (Market Tab)
  • Aesthetic VIP UI with Particle FX
  • Progress Saving (Persistent Balance)
  • Future Prestige System (Coming Soon)
